# Project Overview

## Project Summary
The project is a comprehensive Business Directory and Search Platform (سامانه معرفی و جست‌وجوی کسب‌وکارها) designed to help users find local businesses and services. It includes a robust location-based search, business profile management, designer portfolios, a business card generator, and a subscription-based monetization model.

## Business Objectives
- Provide a centralized platform for businesses to establish a digital presence.
- Enable end-users to easily discover local businesses based on geographical proximity (radius search), categories, and specific filters.
- Monetize the platform via subscription plans and sponsored advertisements.
- Facilitate connections between businesses and designers for custom business card designs.

## Main Users
1. **End Users (Guests/Registered)**: Search for businesses, view profiles, navigate via maps.
2. **Business Owners**: Manage their business profiles, generate basic business cards, purchase subscriptions, and upload existing print designs.
3. **Designers**: Create professional portfolios, upload design samples, and receive referrals/assignments from businesses.
4. **Administrators**: Manage platform content, approve/reject businesses and designer portfolios, oversee subscriptions, ads, and system security.

## Main Features (MVP Scope)
- OTP-based Authentication (SMS).
- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).
- Business Profile Management (Details, Location, Socials, Services).
- Geospatial Search (PostGIS) with Map integration.
- Simple Business Card Generator (minimum 3 standard templates) with QR code.
- Designer Portfolios & Referral system.
- Subscription Plans & Access Control.
- Sponsored Advertisements Showcase.
- Centralized Admin Dashboard.

## Success Criteria
- Delivery of all 7 defined phases within the 45 working days timeline.
- Successful passing of Acceptance Criteria in the Staging/Testing environment for each phase.
- Secure, scalable MVP ready for production deployment within the agreed 48,000,000 Toman budget.
- Seamless integration with the Karlanser platform for milestone payments.
